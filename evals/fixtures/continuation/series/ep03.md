# THE SECOND WILL - EP 03: The Tin
Language: en · Target: 70 s

## S01 - INT. THE SHOP - NIGHT
Cast: MIRA, JUN

Rain on the front window. Mira at the sewing machine, finishing a hem. Jun leans in the doorway, his right hand bandaged, holding a paper bag of takeaway with his left.

JUN: Brought noodles. Peace offering.
MIRA: We're not at war.
JUN: Good. Then they're just noodles.

He puts the bag on the cutting table. Looks around the shop like he's measuring it.

JUN: The buyer wants to see the place Saturday. Just a look. Nothing's decided.
MIRA: Saturday.
JUN: You don't have to be here.
MIRA: [beat] I'll be here.

Jun smiles, taps the door frame twice, and leaves. The bell rings behind him.

## S02 - INT. THE SHOP - NIGHT (CONTINUOUS)
Cast: MIRA

Mira stands. She reaches for the sewing tin on the shelf above the machine, the way she has a thousand times, for a button. Under the buttons: a folded document. She opens it. Grandfather's handwriting. Black ink. Dated two weeks before he died.

She reads the last line. She looks at the door.

She folds the will, puts it back under the buttons, and returns the tin to the shelf. She sits back down at the machine and finishes the hem.

**Ending beat:** decision.
