# Series bible: THE SECOND WILL

- **Logline:** When her grandfather dies, a quiet tailor discovers her charming cousin forged a second will that hands him the family's tailoring shop, and she decides to take it back without letting him know she knows.
- **Genre / pleasure:** family betrayal drama / watching a patient woman outmanoeuvre a confident liar
- **Tone:** restrained, tense, occasionally dry humour
- **Language:** English (neutral), set in a mid-sized coastal city
- **Format:** vertical 9:16, 60–75 s episodes

## Characters
| Name | Role | Want | Flaw | Voice |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| MIRA (31) | Tailor, raised by her grandfather | Keep the shop and expose Jun | Never asks for help | Short sentences, precise, dry |
| JUN (34) | Cousin, salesman, charming | Sell the shop to developers | Overconfident, talks too much when nervous | Warm, joking, fills silences |
| AUNTIE SOL (60) | Grandfather's younger sister | Keep the family from tearing apart | Avoids conflict | Gentle, indirect, uses proverbs |
| MR. HADI (55) | Family lawyer | Stay out of trouble | Cowardly | Formal, hedging |

## Locations
- **The shop:** narrow, fabric bolts on the left wall, cutting table in the center, sewing machine by the front window, back room with a small kitchen. Grandfather's old tin of buttons ("the sewing tin") on the shelf above the machine.

## Reveals plan
- The forged will was witnessed by Mr. Hadi under pressure (planted ep 2, payoff ep 6).
- Grandfather called Auntie Sol the night he died (planted ep 3, payoff ep 5).

## Episode log

## STATE AFTER EP 03
- Timeline: Thursday night, three days after the funeral. Raining.
- Mira KNOWS the second will is forged and HAS the original will (put it back in the sewing tin, on the shelf above the machine).
- Jun does NOT know Mira found the original. He believes Mira has accepted the will.
- Jun's right hand is bandaged (cut on a broken glass at the funeral reception, ep 01).
- Auntie Sol knows Grandfather called her the night he died, but has told no one.
- Mira–Jun: polite on the surface. Mira has decided not to confront him yet.
- Jun's buyer (a developer) wants to see the shop on Saturday.
- Last ending types: reveal, emotional landing, decision.
