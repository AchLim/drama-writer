# Example: a five-episode serial pitch

An original pack for `references/serial-pitch.md`. Each entry is shortened for space; a real pack runs 120 to 200 words per episode.

**Title:** The Second Fitting
**Genre:** Domestic thriller
**Logline:** A bridal-shop seamstress realises the dress she is hemming is for her own husband's second wedding, next Saturday, and that he told the bride his first wife is dead.

**Main characters**
- **Maren Voss** (seamstress, 36): wants her shop and her name back; the lease and her savings are tied to her husband.
- **Tobias Voss** (her husband): wants the money to keep moving; charming, always the one who is "travelling".
- **Celine Hart** (the bride, 29): wants to be married by Saturday; has a reason she is not asking questions.
- **Odette** (shop owner, 60s): wants the shop open; she hired Maren and signed the lease with her.

| Ep | Key event | Ending (the event) | Type |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Maren pins a hem and sees the groom's photo on the bride's phone: Tobias, who left that morning "for Lisbon". She keeps pinning. Celine mentions he is a widower. | Celine says he is collecting the dress tonight and wants to meet the seamstress. | Crisis |
| 2 | Maren hides behind the fitting curtain and hears Tobias charm Celine. He pays the deposit with the joint-account card. She photographs him with the card in his hand. | Maren tells Odette she will finish the dress herself, alone, at night. | Choice |
| 3 | Sewing at midnight, Maren calls the bank about the card and learns of a loan in her name with her signature on it, secured on the shop's lease. | The loan falls due Friday. The shop is seized if it is not repaid. | Crisis |
| 4 | Maren tells Celine in the shop, alone. Celine is calm: she found the ring in his coat a month ago and booked the fitting to see the wife. | Celine says she needs Maren to get Tobias to say it in front of his mother. | Relationship |
| 5 | At the rehearsal dinner, Maren's veil carries a phone. Ruth, Tobias's mother, asks where the money went. Tobias answers, on the recording. | He says it is already in Lisbon, where another woman is waiting for next Saturday. | Secret |

**Chain table**

| Ep | Ending | The next episode opens by |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Tobias is coming tonight | Maren in the back room, listening through the curtain |
| 2 | She will finish the dress alone | Midnight, thread and the bank's hold music |
| 3 | The loan is due Friday | Maren walking into the shop with the facts; Celine answers first |
| 4 | Celine wants Tobias to say it in front of his mother | The dinner table, the veil and its phone |

## What it does well

- **The chain holds.** Each ending is the next opening. Nothing resets and no episode could swap places.
- **Specific events.** "Pays the deposit with the joint-account card" is an image. "Money problems emerge" would not be.
- **Ep 1 opens inside the crisis.** A hem, a phone photo, and a lie about a dead wife, all in the first scene.
- **The price rises.** Maren starts by hiding, then finds her signature on a loan, then must act by Friday.
- **Ending types rotate**: crisis, choice, crisis, relationship, secret.
- **Ep 4 changes who holds the knowledge.** Celine already knew, which turns the bride from a victim into an ally.

## Weak vs strong ending

- **Weak (Ep 2):** "Maren starts to suspect that something bigger is going on." This names no move, no target and no object, so the next episode could start anywhere.
- **Strong (Ep 2):** "Maren tells Odette she will finish the dress herself, alone, at night." It is a choice with a cost, and it sets the opening of Ep 3.

## Rewrite pair (concreteness test)

- Abstract: "Maren and Celine develop an uneasy bond."
- Concrete: "Celine pays for the fitting in cash and asks Maren to keep the receipt, so that nothing about the bride ever appears on a bank statement Tobias can read."
