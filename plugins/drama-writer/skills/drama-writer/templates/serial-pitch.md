# Serial pitch template

For serial pitch mode (`references/serial-pitch.md`). Prose synopses, 120 to 200 words per episode. No scene IDs, shot IDs, camera specs or performance tags. If a platform's brief has its own form, use theirs.

```markdown
# <TITLE>

**Genre:** <e.g. thriller / family drama / revenge>
**Logline:** <who, in what situation, facing what conflict, and the mechanism that makes it unlike the generic version. One or two sentences.>

## Main characters
- **<NAME>** (<role>): <what they want, what blocks them, how they relate to the others>
- **<NAME>** (<role>): <...>

## Overall story
<How it begins, the main conflict, and where it is headed. Show the direction; do not outline every beat.>

## Episodes

### EP.01 | <Plain-description title>
**Opening scene hook:** <the first thing the viewer sees and hears, in one concrete sentence>
<Situation, key event, conflict and change, in prose.>
**Ending / cliffhanger:** <the stated event, and the question it leaves>

### EP.02 | <Title>
<Situation, key event, conflict and change.>
**Ending / cliffhanger:** <...>

### EP.03 | <Title>
<...>
**Ending / cliffhanger:** <...>
```

Add one entry per episode up to the requested count. Add free/paid tags or a paywall block only when the user asks for them.
